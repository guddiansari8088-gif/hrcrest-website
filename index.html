About us

HR Crest Management Solution is a trusted HR consulting and management firm based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, dedicated to delivering comprehensive, end-to-end HR solutions that empower businesses to build strong, efficient, and high-performing teams.

We believe that people are the foundation of every successful organization. With a team of experienced HR professionals, we provide strategic and customized HR services designed to meet the unique needs of businesses across industries.

From talent acquisition and recruitment to payroll management, statutory compliance, employee lifecycle management, HR operations, and workforce consulting, we offer a complete range of HR solutions under one roof.

Our approach combines industry expertise, efficient processes, and a people-centric mindset to help organizations streamline their HR functions, enhance workforce productivity, and achieve sustainable business growth.

At HR Crest Management Solution, our mission is to simplify HR management, strengthen organizations, and create lasting value through reliable, result-driven, and end-to-end HR solutions.

Our hiring process

A clear process from the first call to the first day at work.

  1. Understand your need

    We learn about the role, your team and your budget.

  2. Source candidates

    We look for suitable profiles through our network and job portals.

  3. Screen and shortlist

    We check skills, experience and fit, then share a shortlist with you.

  4. Interviews

    We schedule interviews between you and the shortlisted candidates.

  5. Offer and joining

    We help with offer letters and joining formalities.

  6. Follow-up

    We stay in touch after joining so the new hire settles in well.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what clients and job seekers ask us most.

What services does HR Crest offer?

We offer recruitment and selection, training and development, payroll management, HR policy formation and documentation, statutory compliance, workforce consulting, and HRMS implementation.

Which industries do you work with?

We work with IT and software, manufacturing, service industry, and hardware and software companies.

What types of hiring do you handle?

We handle permanent hiring, contract staffing, executive search and campus recruitment.

What does payroll management include?

It includes salary processing, payslip generation, attendance integration and payroll compliance.

Which statutory compliances do you take care of?

We handle PF compliance, ESIC compliance, labour law compliance and shop and establishment registration.

Can I take just one service, or do I need the full HR package?

You can choose a single service or a complete HR package. Tell us what you need and we will suggest the right option.

How much do your services cost?

The cost depends on the services you choose and the size of your team. Contact us with your requirement and we will share a quote.

How do I get started?

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 8882156985, email info@hrcrest.in, or fill in the contact form. We will get back to you to understand your needs.

I am looking for a job. How do I apply?

Fill in the form in the Careers section, upload your resume and submit it. Our team receives your application.

What is HR outsourcing?

HR outsourcing means handing over some or all of your HR work, such as hiring, payroll and compliance, to an expert team like ours, so you can focus on running your business.

Do you help with employee handbooks and HR policies?

Yes. We prepare employee handbooks, HR policies and SOPs, appointment letters and a complete HR documentation framework.

Can you set up HR software (HRMS) for us?

Yes. We help with HR software setup, attendance and leave management, employee database management and process automation.

Do you provide training for employees and managers?

Yes. Our training and development services include soft skills training, leadership development, employee engagement programs and performance improvement.

What is workforce consulting?

It covers organization structure design, manpower planning, performance management systems and HR strategy development, so your team is planned and organized well.

What is campus recruitment?

Campus recruitment means hiring fresh talent directly from colleges and institutes. It is part of our recruitment and selection service.

What is contract staffing?

Contract staffing means hiring people for a fixed period or a specific project instead of as permanent employees. We help you find suitable candidates for such roles.

Can freshers apply through HR Crest?

Yes. Select Fresher in the experience field of the Careers form and tell us the role or field you are looking for.

What should I send when applying for a job?

Please send your updated resume. Adding your phone number and the role you are looking for helps us contact you and understand what you need.
